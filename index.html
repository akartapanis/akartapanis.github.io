I am an Associate Professor, with tenure, in the James Benjamin Department of Accounting at the Mays Business School at Texas A&M University. My research focuses on the intersection of law and accounting, including securities litigation, fraud, and misreporting. My work has been published in accounting journals, including the Journal of Accounting and Economics, The Accounting Review, and Contemporary Accounting Research; finance journals, including the Journal of Financial and Quantitative Analysis; and law and economics journals, including the Journal of Law and Economics.

I currently teach Financial Statement Analysis combined with Data Analytics. The course is centered on academic papers. Students first study the papers and then replicate their main findings. This format helps students deepen their understanding of accounting concepts and statistics while also introducing them to coding.
